Provenance-gated payments for AI agents

Move beyond blind trust. Pay only what has provenance.

Lineage traces where every payee, address and amount came from, and holds anything that originated in an untrusted email, chat or memory. The rule runs outside the model, so even a jailbroken agent can’t pay an attacker.

The idea

Every value has a birthplace. Lineage makes the agent show its papers before a single cent moves.

Everything you need to stop a bad payment.

Guardrails that cap amounts and keep allowlists don’t ask the one question that matters: where did this payee come from, and is that source allowed to set it? Lineage does.

PROVENANCE

Label at the door, trace at the gate

Every email, chat message, memory entry and tool result becomes a fragment with a trust tier assigned by our code. Before payment, the payee is traced back through the context to the exact message that put it there.

See a trace →

BINDING

Bound to records, not to the agent’s say-so

The invoice, purchase order and vendor come from the database. The amount can’t exceed the approved PO, and a payee that belongs to another vendor is rejected, the confused-deputy case a plain allowlist waves through.

See the ablations →

OWNER IN THE LOOP

Holds become approvals, approvals become evidence

An untrusted change turns into an owner approval that shows its lineage. The executor only pays the exact arguments that were decided, once, and every step lands in a hash-chained audit log you can verify.

What it doesn’t do

  • Tracing matches identifiers exactly. It doesn’t follow derived values, so amounts are bounded by the PO instead.
  • A poisoned vendor master or a compromised owner channel is out of scope.
  • The benchmark is small and author-written, and its attacker is simulated. It shows a deterministic enforcement point, not a proof of security.
