The agent that pays your invoices can’t be phished.

How it works

Every value has a birthplace.

01

Label at the door

Every email, chat message, memory entry and tool result becomes a fragment with a trust tier assigned by our code. The model can never create or upgrade a label.

02

Trace the value

Before money moves, the payee is traced through the context. If it only appears in untrusted text, we know exactly which message put it there.

03

Decide outside the model

The guard answers ALLOW, ASK_OWNER or BLOCK, binds the amount to the approved PO, and writes a hash-chained audit record.

What it doesn’t do

  • Tracing matches identifiers exactly. It doesn’t follow derived values, so amounts are bounded by the PO instead.
  • A poisoned vendor master or a compromised owner channel is out of scope.
  • The benchmark is small and author-written, and its attacker is simulated. It shows a deterministic enforcement point, not a proof of security.
